A little civics detour

WAIT. DIDN'T WE JUST REDRAW THESE?

Texas redrew its U.S. congressional districts in 2025, between the usual once-a-decade redistricting cycles. Your congressional district for 2026 may differ from the one you knew before. Congressional boundaries are distinct from Texas House, Texas Senate, school-board, and local precinct boundaries. Check your actual sample ballot.
