01 / MORE EARLY CHECK-INS CAME FROM BLUE AREAS.

Historically Harris-voting areas contributed 58,202 early check-ins (60.9%), compared with 37,440 (39.1%) in Trump-voting areas. Those are totals across all classified groups—not Democratic or Republican votes.

2024 Harris-voting areas · check-ins58,20260.9% of classified check-ins
2024 Trump-voting areas · check-ins37,44039.1% of classified check-ins

Why? The historically blue areas contain many more registered voters. A red area's turnout rate can be higher even while blue areas contribute more actual voters.

SO WHAT DOES THIS TELL US?

Imagine two neighborhoods invited to a party. We're counting who shows up.

The red neighborhood had a higher turnout rate (60% versus 45%). But the blue neighborhood brought more people (nine versus six), because it started out bigger.

That's what's happening in Hays County: historically red areas had higher early-participation rates, while more total voters checked in from historically blue areas, which have more registered voters. Blue areas also saw bigger gains since 2022.

Remember: These colors describe how an area voted in 2024—not how anyone voted in 2026.

HOW WE DID THE MATH / READ THE FINE PRINT

The chart covers 47 populated, reconstructed geographic areas, some combining multiple current precincts because boundaries changed. The 58,202 and 37,440 group totals sum to 95,642 check-ins, one fewer than the 95,643 countywide total; one check-in is not assigned to a classified group. The first chart ranks group counts, so group size affects its order. The median values describe geographic areas—not individuals or party affiliation. 2026 precinct rates use July registered-voter counts as provisional denominators. Historical early-ballot reports and 2026 check-in records may differ in coverage or certification; mail ballots are excluded. The countywide 95,643 total counts distinct voter identifiers after removing six duplicate entries. Comparisons are descriptive, not official final turnout.

Primary comparisons use the same reconstructed groups in both years, not full countywide totals. Official 2026 countywide primary ballots were 34,049 Democratic and 18,686 Republican. An area's 2024 presidential result does not reveal how anyone voted in 2026.
