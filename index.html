Your Election Day field guide

WHERE DO I VOTE TUESDAY?

Tuesday, November 3 · 7 AM–7 PM. In Hays County, you can vote at any Election Day vote center in the county, not just one assigned precinct. But check the current location list before you leave — not every early-voting site is necessarily open on Election Day.

Find a location using the official Texas voter lookup ↗ or check San Marcos election-day voting locations ↗. Bring an accepted photo ID, or check Texas ID options ↗ if you need an alternative.
